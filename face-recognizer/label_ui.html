People

    Add photos

    Type or paste the folder that holds the new photos and videos (subfolders are included). Photos and videos already added, and copies of them, are skipped, and so are Live Photo clips. In a video, about one frame a second is looked at and each person's few clearest faces are kept. Faces the model is sure about are named for you.

    Rename

    Using a name that already exists merges the two people.

    Their data

    What this tool holds about them, all on this computer: their faces' prints and pictures, their labels and the labeling history, saved Web search answers, photo tags and descriptions that name them, Collect faces pictures, presence log visits, Expression test frames and feeling reports.

    Export

    Writes all of it into a new folder inside the one you give: data.json, their face pictures and Collect faces pictures.

    Forget

    Deletes all of it and removes their name everywhere. Their faces stay as Ignored faces without print or picture, so their photos aren't read again. Undo is cleared, and this can't be undone. In the app window the Face tracking tab also forgets their usual look and normal signals; with the tracker open in a browser, use Forget what's learned there.

    Web search

    Settings and other ways to search

    Web search sends this face to Amazon (celebrity recognition) and this photo to Google (pages with the same picture), both US companies, and keeps English results from US sites only. The pictures leave this computer and may be kept by them. Each service stops itself before $10 a month.

    The keys are your own (how to make them: the Web search section of the README) and stay in %APPDATA%\FaceLabels on this computer. Save an empty key to remove it.
