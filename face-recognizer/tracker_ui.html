Expression test

Measures how well expression reading works on your own face and webcam. You're asked to make each of the 7 expressions for a few seconds. Only numbers are saved (the expression asked for, the model's scores and face-shape cues; no pictures) in data\expr_eval. Run tools\expr_eval.py to see the results.

Display

Expression is read from sharp, camera-facing frames and averaged over the last second. With calibration on, each named person's usual look is learned over about a minute; a reading that departs from it is highlighted, one that matches it is marked "usual".
