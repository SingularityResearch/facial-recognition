    "Arrive": first seen after being out of view at least that long. "Leave": out of view that long. "Unusual": any of their signals that many spreads from their normal for that long (needs "Calibrate to each person"; these thresholds are guesses, not yet measured).       Expression test

      Measures how well expression reading works on your own face and webcam. You're asked to make each of the 7 expressions for a few seconds, then to talk for a few seconds and to rest your chin on your hand (to measure the talking and hand detectors). Only numbers are saved (the expression asked for, the model's scores, face-shape cues, body points and talking signals; no pictures, no audio) in data\expr_eval. Run tools\expr_eval.py to see the results.

      Feeling check

      The only way to learn whether any of these signals track how you actually feel: now and then you say how you feel (two 1-7 scales). tools\expr_eval.py compares the answers with the signals and, once there are enough, fits valence/arousal to you.

      Display

      Expression is read from sharp, camera-facing frames and averaged over the last second. With calibration on, each named person's usual look is learned over about a minute; a reading that departs from it is highlighted, one that matches it is marked "usual". Calibration also learns each person's normal for every other signal (posture, movement, voice, pulse...), alone and in company, over a few minutes, and the evidence shows what is unusual for them. It is kept on this computer (and in the shared database, if one is set up, so your other devices know it too); it never goes anywhere else.

      Voice is analysed live and never recorded: loudness, pitch, speaking rate, and the voice model's arousal/valence. Pulse reads tiny skin-colour changes and needs a still face in steady light. Body posture adds head height, shoulders, lean, body movement, breathing, hand on face and crossed arms. The "skip" and "state model" options change the expression reading and aren't measured yet; run the Expression test and tools\expr_eval.py first.

      While a face stays in view, a sharp picture of that face is saved every few seconds (up to 12 per face) with who the tracker thinks it is and how sure: just the face with a little room around it, never the whole frame, and any other face in that room greyed out.
